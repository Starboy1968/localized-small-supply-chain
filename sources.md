# Sources and claims to verify

This guide is built mostly from method, not from facts about the world. It uses **no statistics, studies or real company names**. Every business, supplier, vendor, item and number in the examples is fictional. The few factual claims it does make are listed below.

## Checked sources
- **Odoo Community is free and open source (LGPLv3), and includes an Inventory module.** Odoo's own repository: [LICENSE](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/master/LICENSE) ("Odoo is published under the GNU LESSER GENERAL PUBLIC LICENSE, Version 3") and the Inventory (`stock`) module manifest, `'license': 'LGPL-3'` ([addons/stock/__manifest__.py](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/master/addons/stock/__manifest__.py)). Checked Oct 2026.
- **Mistral's chat assistant has a free plan; "Le Chat" is now called "Vibe."** [Mistral: Vibe (formerly Le Chat)](https://mistral.ai/products/vibe/) and [Mistral pricing](https://mistral.ai/pricing/). Checked Oct 2026.

## Claims to verify
Items below are common knowledge or came from search summaries. Check before quoting.
- [ ] **Free plans** for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Meta AI, Mistral's assistant and Microsoft Copilot: all had free plans as of Oct 2026 per vendor pages and search results, but limits and features change often.
- [ ] **Meta AI runs on Llama models:** from third-party reviews and search summaries, not from a Meta spec sheet.
- [ ] **"Many AI tools let you turn off use of your chats for training":** true for several major tools as of Oct 2026, but the setting's name and location differ and change. Check your own tool.
- [ ] **"Most POS systems can export sales by item as CSV":** common, but not universal. Check your own system's reports or export menu.
- [ ] **Spreadsheet functions** (`COUNTIFS`, `MINIFS`, `MAXIFS`, date subtraction): standard in Google Sheets and recent desktop spreadsheets; older versions may differ.
- [ ] **Vendor holiday slowdowns:** described as something to ask about, not as a fact about any vendor.

## Not covered on purpose
- Food-safety, alcohol, medical-supply, tax and legal rules. These vary by place; check the official source or a professional.
- Formula-based forecasting or risk scoring. This guide keeps to plain words and simple arithmetic.
